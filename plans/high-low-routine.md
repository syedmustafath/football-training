# High/Low Football Comeback Routine
*Home-based, no gym, ~30 min per session. Built around Monday 5v5 football and a 12-week 5k goal.*

**Equipment:** a sturdy backpack loaded with books/water bottles (your "barbell"), a towel, a sofa or partner to anchor your heels, and a wall. Optional: a resistance band.

---

## The Week at a Glance

| Day | Intensity | Session | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | HIGH | 5v5 football | — |
| Tue | LOW | Upper + core **+ ball work** | 30 + 15 min |
| Wed | LOW | 5k progression run | 25–40 min |
| Thu | HIGH | Lower strength + jumps | 30 min |
| Fri | LOW | Upper + mobility **+ ball work** | 25 + 15 min |
| Sat | MODERATE | Speed & strides | 25 min |
| Sun | OFF | Full rest | — |

Ball work is skill practice, not physical training — it adds almost no fatigue, so it lives on low days without disturbing the high/low rhythm. Do it *before* the strength session while you're fresh; touch quality drops fast when tired. It can also stand alone as its own mini-session earlier in the day.

The logic: your two hardest days (Mon, Thu) are separated by 72 hours in each direction. Saturday speed work is short and sharp enough that you arrive fresh for Monday football.

---

## Tuesday — Upper + Core (LOW)

**Warm-up (4 min):** arm circles, band pull-aparts or wall slides, 10 slow scapular push-ups, 20 jumping jacks.

**Main block — 3 rounds, minimal rest between exercises, 60–90 sec between rounds (~22 min):**

1. Press-up — 8–12 reps (progress: feet elevated → tempo 3-sec lowering)
2. Towel row — 10–12 reps (towel around a door handle or table edge, lean back, row)
3. Pike press-up — 6–10 reps (shoulders)
4. Backpack biceps curl to overhead press — 10 reps
5. Side plank — 30–40 sec each side
6. Dead bug — 8 slow reps each side

**Finish (2 min):** doorway chest stretch, thoracic rotations.

---

## Wednesday — 5k Progression Run (LOW)

Run at conversational pace — if you can't talk, slow down. One run per week is enough; football covers the rest of your conditioning.

| Weeks | Session |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | 8 × (run 2 min / walk 2 min) |
| 3–4 | 6 × (run 3 min / walk 90 sec) |
| 5–6 | 5 × (run 5 min / walk 90 sec) |
| 7–8 | 3 × (run 8 min / walk 2 min) |
| 9–10 | Run 15 min / walk 3 min / run 10 min |
| 11 | Run 25 min continuous |
| 12 | **5k — run/walk allowed, just finish** |

If a week feels hard, repeat it before moving on. Missing a week and repeating beats forcing a jump.

---

## Thursday — Lower Strength + Jumps (HIGH)

*This is your most important session of the week. Always start single-leg exercises with the surgical leg, and match reps on the other side — never do more on the strong leg.*

**Warm-up (5 min):** 20 bodyweight squats, 10 walking lunges, 10 pogo hops in place (soft, quiet landings), leg swings.

**Block 1 — Strength, 3 rounds (~15 min):**

1. Rear-foot-elevated split squat (back foot on sofa/chair, backpack on) — 8 each leg
2. Single-leg Romanian deadlift (backpack in opposite hand) — 8 each leg
3. Nordic curl regression — 5 slow lowers (heels anchored under sofa, lower as slowly as possible, push back up with hands). Non-negotiable — this is your hamstring insurance.
4. Single-leg calf raise — 15–20 each leg (off a step if available)

**Block 2 — Jump progression, 3 sets (~7 min).** Quality over quantity; stop while landings still feel crisp.

| Weeks | Drill |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Squat jump to stick landing — 5 reps. Hold each landing 2 sec, silent feet, knee tracking over toes. |
| 5–8 | Single-leg hop to stick — 4 each leg, forward and lateral. |
| 9–12 | Lateral bounds (skater jumps) — 5 each side, stick each landing. |

**Loading rule:** when 8 reps of a strength exercise feels comfortable, add weight to the backpack or slow the lowering to 3 seconds. Something must get harder every 1–2 weeks.

---

## Friday — Upper + Mobility (LOW)

**Upper (12 min), 2 relaxed rounds:** press-up variation × 10, towel row × 10, kneeling superman × 8 each side, glute bridge × 12 (light hamstring/glute flush, not to fatigue).

**Mobility (12 min):** couch stretch (hip flexors) 60 sec/side · 90/90 hip rotations × 8/side · ankle wall rocks × 12/side (drive the knee past toes — critical for the surgical side) · hamstring floss 45 sec/side · deep squat hold 2 × 45 sec.

---

## Tuesday & Friday — Ball Work (15 min, before the main session)

All you need is a ball and 2–3 metres of space; a wall unlocks the best drills. Set a timer per drill and move on — frequency and total touches beat perfection.

**Block 1 — Juggling (5 min):**
- Weeks 1–4: thigh juggles and single-foot lift-and-catch. Target: 10 consecutive thigh touches, then 5 consecutive foot touches per foot.
- Weeks 5–8: continuous feet-only juggling. Target: beat your record each week.
- Weeks 9–12: pattern juggling — right, left, thigh, repeat. Add a "juggle → catch on foot → hold 2 sec" finisher (doubles as quiet single-leg balance work for the surgical side).

**Block 2 — Wall passing (5 min):**
- 2-touch: pass against the wall, control with one touch, return with the next. 60 sec right foot, 60 sec left foot.
- 1-touch: 60 sec each foot, softer pace, focus on clean contact.
- Weeks 5+: control with one foot, pass with the other; vary distance 2–5 m.

**Block 3 — Close control (5 min):**
- Toe taps on the ball — 30 sec (light, quick).
- Sole rolls side-to-side — 60 sec.
- Figure-8 dribbling around two markers (shoes work) — 2 min, both feet, inside and outside of the boot.
- La croqueta / inside-inside chops between the feet — 60 sec.

**Optional daily habit:** 5 minutes of juggling any day, including rest days — it's the single fastest way to rebuild first touch, and it costs nothing physically.

*One caution: keep juggling volume modest in weeks 1–4 if the surgical knee gets grumpy — thousands of tiny single-leg stance moments add up. The daily 5 minutes is plenty.*

---

## Saturday — Speed & Strides (MODERATE)

Short and crisp. You should finish feeling energised, not tired.

**Warm-up (8 min):** easy jog 3 min, then A-skips 2 × 15 m, high knees 2 × 15 m, side shuffles 2 × 15 m each direction.

**Main (12 min):** 6 strides of 60–80 m, building to ~85% of top speed, walking back for full recovery. Focus on tall posture and relaxed acceleration — no all-out sprints for the first 4 weeks; add 2 harder efforts from week 5 if the knee feels good.

**Optional (3 min):** 4 × gentle 45° cuts at jogging pace each side — rehearses Monday's movements at low risk.

---

## Rules of the Road

1. **The 24-hour rule:** if the knee is swollen or achy the morning after any session, that session was too much. Drop one set or one intensity step next time.
2. **Asymmetry check:** every 4 weeks, do 3 single-leg hops for distance on each leg. If the surgical leg reaches under ~90% of the other side, keep loading Thursday's single-leg work before progressing jumps.
3. **Never skip:** Nordics (Thu) and the ankle/hip mobility (Fri). Everything else can flex around a busy week.
4. **Bad week protocol:** if work fatigue wins, the priority order is Thu > Wed > Sat > Tue > Fri. Protect Thursday at all costs. Ball work is the exception — it's cheap enough to keep even on a bad week, and 5 minutes of juggling on the balcony still counts.
